# SO-101 reference model

Source: https://github.com/TheRobotStudio/SO-ARM100
Local source revision: `5f6d2b876a53a4872e405b991dd925556c9e38a4` (copied from the sibling SO-ARM100 checkout).
Original URDF: `Simulation/SO101/so101_new_calib.urdf`.
Meshes: the 13 STL files referenced by that URDF, from its `assets/` directory.
License: Apache-2.0; preserved in `LICENSE-SO-ARM100`.
Original generated URDF attribution comments are retained.

Modification: mesh paths use `package://pinchy_description/meshes/` so the
installed ROS package is self-contained. Geometry, joints, limits, and inertias
are otherwise unchanged. This is the stock follower with its original gripper,
not the custom writing arm. It has five positioning joints plus a gripper joint.
The new calibration places joint zero at the middle of its range.

Validate collision coverage, joint limits, calibration, and inertias against the
built arm before using this reference for planning or hardware control.

## CAD 4 custom model

`urdf/pinchy_arm.urdf` is generated by `tools/build_arm_model.py` from the
owner-supplied `cad/cad4/iteration_2_cad_4_arm.urdf` and its provenance-matched
`source/cad4_model.xml`. The source archive, original exports, reports and
SHA-256 manifest are preserved. The stock SO-101 geometry retains the upstream
Apache-2.0 attribution shown above. CAD 4 joint six is `pen_pitch`, and the
nominal pen tip assumes 5 mm spring compression.

Stock STL visuals and source analysis inertias are merged into the runtime
model. Pen-holder shapes use the source MJCF's simplified box/cylinder/capsule
geometry. `meshes/cad4/wrist_roll_follower_so101_noclaw.stl` is a reconstructed
proxy made by `tools/build_noclaw_mesh.py`, cutting the upstream stock follower
at the native-part z=38 mm plane specified in `cad4_parameters.json`. The
original source analysis NoClaw STL was not included in the bundle. The
reconstruction is a derivative of the upstream Apache-2.0 mesh.

`cad/EE_STEP_V1` and `meshes/ee_v1` retain the earlier owner-supplied individual
STEP parts and their millimetre STL conversions. They are separate from the
new source model and still available in the inspection gallery. See
[CAD 4 model notes](../../docs/cad4_model.md) for integration and validation.
